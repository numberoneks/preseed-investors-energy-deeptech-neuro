Neuroscience and psychedelic medicine investors (2026)

Early investors in neuroscience and psychedelic medicine: who backed Gilgamesh Pharmaceuticals and Beckley Psytech, plus pre-seed health and biotech funds from an open dataset. Data: CSV · Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24239379 · Hugging Face (CC BY 4.0).

How this list was built: The dataset has few neuroscience tags, so this page shows three things. First, Ayuh Ventures (disclosed). Second, the backers of Gilgamesh Pharmaceuticals and Beckley Psytech from the site's Groq Test, each with its source. Third, dataset funds tagged both Health and Biotech that write pre-seed checks. Those are broader health and biotech funds, not neuroscience specialists.

  2. Aera VC: In Gilgamesh's $2M seed (July 2020) (source)
  3. Prime Movers Lab: Led Gilgamesh's May 2021 round; joined Beckley Psytech's Series B (source)
  4. Noetic Fund: In Beckley Psytech's Series A (December 2020) (source)
  5. Bicycle Day Ventures: Beckley Psytech investor from the Series A (source)
  6. Palo Santo: In both the Gilgamesh and Beckley Psytech syndicates (source)

Outcome: AbbVie completed its acquisition of Gilgamesh's lead drug bretisilocin (October 2025). Beckley Psytech combined with atai into AtaiBeckley (November 2025), and Lilly completed its acquisition of AtaiBeckley (September 2026). (source)

Where does this data come from?

Each row links to a public source, mostly VC Sheet fund profiles fetched 2026-11-14. The dataset (152 US funds, CC BY 4.0) is at https://preseed-investors.vercel.app/data/preseed_investors_us_2026.csv, archived on Zenodo with DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24239379 (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24239379) and mirrored on Hugging Face (https://huggingface.co/datasets/numberoneks/preseed-investors-us-2026). Confirm a fund's terms with the fund before you pitch.

Is the maintainer of this list an investor?

Yes. The dataset is maintained by Kunal Sethi, General Partner of Prithvi Ventures and Ayuh Ventures in New York. Both firms invest at incubation, pre-seed and seed with $500K–$1M checks. Their entries are self-reported, and they're listed separately from the ranked funds.

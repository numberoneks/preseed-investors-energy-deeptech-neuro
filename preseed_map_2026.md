# The 2026 Pre-Seed Map: Energy, Deeptech and Neuro Investors
*By Kunal Sethi · DRAFT for writing.numberoneks.com · not published*

Founders keep asking me the same question: who actually writes the first check in energy, deeptech and neuroscience? So here is my map. It holds 50 funds I'd point a founder to, each with the reason and a public source, plus the full open dataset of 150 US pre-seed funds behind it.

**How I ranked it.** The order comes from fit for a pre-seed founder in these sectors: sector match, a check range that overlaps $500K to $1M, leading pre-seed, a New York presence, and how many founder-cited VC Sheet lists the fund appears on. The neuro section ends with eight funds that passed what I call **the Groq Test**: they backed Gilgamesh Pharmaceuticals or Beckley Psytech before AbbVie and Lilly came in.

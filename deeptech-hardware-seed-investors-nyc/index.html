Where does this data come from?

Each row links to a public source, mostly VC Sheet fund profiles fetched 2026-11-14. The dataset (152 US funds, CC BY 4.0) is at https://preseed-investors.vercel.app/data/preseed_investors_us_2026.csv, archived on Zenodo with DOI 10.5281/zenodo.24239379 (https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.24239379) and mirrored on Hugging Face (https://huggingface.co/datasets/numberoneks/preseed-investors-us-2026). Confirm a fund's terms with the fund before you pitch.

Is the maintainer of this list an investor?

Yes. The dataset is maintained by Kunal Sethi, General Partner of Prithvi Ventures and Ayuh Ventures in New York. Both firms invest at incubation, pre-seed and seed with $500K–$1M checks. Their entries are self-reported, and they're listed separately from the ranked funds.
